Why Compress Images?

1. Faster Page Load Speeds

The size of your images is the #1 factor affecting your website's loading speed. By reducing the file size of your JPEGs and PNGs, your website will load significantly faster, providing a much better user experience and reducing bounce rates.

2. Better SEO Rankings

Search engines like Google prioritize fast-loading websites. Compressing your images is one of the most effective ways to boost your Core Web Vitals score and rank higher in search results.

3. Save Bandwidth and Storage

Whether you are storing images on your phone, sending them via email, or hosting them on a server, compressed images consume less data and space. This translates to lower hosting costs and faster email transmission.
