Detailed Reviews

1. SwiftCompress — Best for Privacy

SwiftCompress compresses images using the Canvas API and WebAssembly directly in your browser. This architecture guarantees your photos never leave your device. Testing on a 5.2MB iPhone photo at 80% quality produced a 487KB output — a 91% reduction — with no visible quality difference at normal viewing sizes.

Verdict: Best choice for sensitive photos (medical, personal, business). The only major tool with true zero-upload architecture and unlimited free usage.

2. TinyPNG — Best Compression Ratio for PNG

TinyPNG uses a proprietary "smart lossy compression" technique that specifically targets PNG files. It uploads your file to their servers, compresses it, and returns a download link. Results are excellent for PNG graphics and logos — often achieving 70–80% compression for PNG files that other tools struggle with.

Verdict: Great results but limited to 20 free compressions per month. Server upload is a privacy concern for personal photos.

3. Squoosh (by Google) — Best for Technical Users

Squoosh is a Google-developed open-source tool with incredible format support: WebP, AVIF, OxiPNG, MozJPEG, and more. It's browser-based (no uploads) and offers the most control over encoding settings. However, its UI is complex and not beginner-friendly.

Verdict: Best if you know what "AVIF" and "quantization" mean. For most users, SwiftCompress's simpler quality slider is more practical.

Our Verdict: Which Tool Should You Use?

  • For personal or sensitive photos → SwiftCompress (zero uploads, unlimited free)
  • For PNG logos and graphics → TinyPNG (best PNG-specific compression)
  • For web developers wanting full control → Squoosh by Google
  • For bulk compression → SwiftCompress (no daily limits)
