Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 30, 2026

At SwiftCompress, privacy is our foundational principle. This policy explains our data practices, which are exceptionally minimal because of the client-side nature of our application.

1. File Processing & Security

We do not upload your files. All file compression and optimization processes (Image, PDF, and Video) are executed locally within your web browser using WebAssembly (WASM). Your original files and the resulting optimized files remain strictly on your device. We do not transmit, store, or have access to any of the files you process using SwiftCompress.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

Because you do not need an account to use our service, we do not collect your name, email address, physical address, or payment information.

3. Google AdSense & Cookies

To keep SwiftCompress 100% free for all users, we use Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google AdSense uses cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to our website or other websites.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to our site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings.

4. GDPR and CCPA Compliance

We respect the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA). Because we do not collect personal file data or require accounts, the scope of personal data processed is limited entirely to standard web analytics and advertising cookies as outlined above.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us via our Contact Page or directly at privacy@swiftcompress.com.
