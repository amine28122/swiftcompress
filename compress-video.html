The Power of In-Browser Video Compression

1. Bypass Platform Restrictions

Social platforms and messaging apps have strict limits. Discord restricts free users to 8MB or 25MB, and WhatsApp limits videos to 16MB. Our tool lets you reduce your MP4 and WebM files to meet these exact requirements without needing to buy premium subscriptions.

2. Save Mobile Data

Sharing raw, uncompressed 4K video directly from your phone consumes massive amounts of mobile data. By compressing the video first, you make it instantly shareable while preserving the visual quality for small screens.

3. WebAssembly (WASM) Technology

We use a compiled version of FFmpeg that runs entirely inside your browser. This means you get professional-grade H.264 encoding locally. Your personal videos are never uploaded to a remote server, ensuring maximum speed and complete privacy.
