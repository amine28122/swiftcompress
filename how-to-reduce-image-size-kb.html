How to Reduce Image Size in KB Online Free — Hit Any Target

SwiftCompress Engineering Team Published June 12, 2026 • 6 min read

Job application portals, visa forms, university admissions, and government websites all have one thing in common: they demand photos under a specific file size. "File must be under 100KB" is a sentence that has frustrated millions of applicants.

This guide explains exactly how to compress any image to your required KB target — whether that's 50KB, 100KB, 200KB, or 500KB — using a free, private browser tool.

Quick Fix

Go to SwiftCompress Image Optimizer → Upload photo → Use the quality slider below to hit your target KB size → Download immediately.

Common Use Cases That Require Specific KB Sizes

Visa & Passport Applications
US VISA (DS-160): Under 50 KB. Schengen Visa: Under 200 KB. UK eVisa: Under 500 KB.
Job Application Portals
LinkedIn profile photo: Under 8MB (but keep under 200KB for faster loading). HR portals: commonly 100–500KB.
University & Scholarship Applications
UCAS, Common App, and most university portals require photos under 100KB–2MB.
Government & Banking Portals
Tax filings, license applications, and bank KYC forms typically require under 200KB–500KB.

Step-by-Step: Compress an Image to Under 100KB

  1. Open SwiftCompress Image Optimizer in any browser.
  2. Click the upload area or drag your photo onto it. Files up to 20MB are accepted.
  3. The tool shows your original file size. Set the quality slider to 50% as a starting point.
  4. Click "Optimize Image" — the compressed size appears immediately. If still over 100KB, click "Process Another," re-upload, and try 40%.
  5. Once under 100KB, click "Download Compressed Image." The JPEG file is ready for your application.
