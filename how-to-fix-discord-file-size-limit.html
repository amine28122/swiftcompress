How to Bypass Discord File Size Limit (Fix 8MB/25MB Free)

SwiftCompress Engineering Team Published June 12, 2026 • 6 min read

You've captured a hilarious gaming moment and want to drop it in your Discord server. You hit upload, and boom — you get the dreaded message: "Your files are too powerful. Max file size is 25.00 MB."

Discord heavily restricts file sizes to encourage users to buy their $9.99/month Nitro subscription. But what if you just want to share a single clip without paying a monthly fee?

The 30-Second Free Fix

Go to SwiftCompress Video Reducer → Upload your clip → Set quality slider to 30% - 40% → Click Compress. Your 150MB video will shrink below 25MB and upload to Discord perfectly.

Discord's Current File Size Limits (2026)

  • Free Users: 25 MB limit (recently increased from the notorious 8MB limit)
  • Nitro Basic ($2.99/mo): 50 MB limit
  • Nitro Full ($9.99/mo): 500 MB limit
  • Server Boost Level 2: 50 MB for all members in that server
  • Server Boost Level 3: 100 MB for all members in that server

If you're recording gameplay via OBS, ShadowPlay, or your smartphone, even a 30-second clip can exceed 50MB. Compression is mandatory.

How to Compress Video for Discord Free

  1. Open SwiftCompress Video Reducer in your browser.
  2. Upload your MP4, WebM, or MOV file.
  3. Crucial Step: Adjust the quality slider. For Discord, you need to use aggressive compression. Set it between 30% and 40%. This tells our FFmpeg engine to prioritize file size reduction.
  4. Click Compress. Because the tool runs in your browser via WebAssembly, it doesn't waste time uploading your massive video to a server.
  5. Download the resulting file. It will be under 25MB and ready to drop directly into Discord.

Watch out for Sketchy "Discord Compressors"

Many sites claiming to compress video for Discord are ad-farms that force you to upload your video to unsecure servers, hit you with pop-ups, and add ugly watermarks to your video. SwiftCompress runs 100% locally in your browser, has no watermarks, and never steals your data.

Bypass the Discord Limit Now

No Nitro required. Compress your video locally in your browser for free.
