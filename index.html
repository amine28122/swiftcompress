Frequently Asked Questions

Is SwiftCompress safe to use?

Yes, absolutely. Unlike other online tools that upload your files to remote servers, SwiftCompress operates entirely within your web browser. This means your files never leave your device, ensuring 100% privacy and security.

Are there any file size limits?

Since processing happens on your device, the maximum file size depends on your computer's RAM and browser capabilities, not on our servers. Generally, you can compress very large files without encountering artificial paywalls.

Which formats are supported?

Currently, we support popular image formats (JPEG, PNG, WebP), standard PDF documents, and MP4/WebM video files. We are continuously adding support for more formats based on user feedback.
